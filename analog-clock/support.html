Analog Clock - Support

For Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac

Analog Clock is an analog clock widget by alpxstudio. Several clock styles are free; premium styles can be unlocked with a one-time purchase.

Contact

For questions, problems or suggestions, write to alpxstudio@gmail.com. Please mention your device, its system version and the app version. We usually reply within two working days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add the widget on iPhone or iPad?

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Analog Clock, choose a size and tap Add Widget.

How do I add the widget on Android?

Touch and hold an empty area of the home screen, tap Widgets, find Analog Clock and drag it to the home screen.

How do I change the clock style?

Open the Analog Clock app, pick a style and apply it. The widget updates automatically.

I bought a premium clock. Will I have it on my other devices?

Yes. Purchases belong to your Apple Account or Google account. Open the app on the other device while signed in to the same account and your clocks unlock automatically. On Apple devices, a clock bought on iPhone or iPad is also unlocked on Mac.

The price is not shown or the purchase button does not work.

Check your internet connection and that in-app purchases are allowed on the device (on iPhone: Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions). Then close and reopen the app.

In some countries, in-app purchases or certain payment methods are not available through the App Store or Google Play. In that case premium clocks cannot be bought from that country, and the free clock styles remain fully usable.
